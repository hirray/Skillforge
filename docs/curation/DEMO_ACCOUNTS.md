## Switching Personas in Development

Use the **Fast Persona Switcher** embedded in the top-bar Context Switcher menu (`Workspace HUD`). Clicking any persona immediately re-binds your active authorization token, adjusts your role context, and streams the appropriate workspace dashboard.
