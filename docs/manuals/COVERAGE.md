# SkillForge 34-Module Coverage Matrix

Every single one of SkillForge's 34 modules is formally mapped below to:
1. **The User Manual Chapter** that explains its concepts, business rules, step-by-step actions, and troubleshooting.
2. **The Flowchart Diagram** (FC-01 through FC-16) that visualizes its exact lifecycle states, decision diamonds, and participant interactions.
3. **The Interactive Guided Spotlight Tour** (T1 through T20, plus Welcome tours) that walks users through the real interface click-by-click.

No module is unmapped.
